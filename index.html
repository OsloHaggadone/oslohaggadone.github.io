About me

I’m Oslo Haggadone. I study Computer Science at UC Santa Cruz, and I make music: since 2023 I’ve released two albums and two singles, starting with Hollow Journey, the soundtrack to a video game.

The two meet in Synopsist, the music website I’m building, where my songs stream for free in a player I made, each one analyzed moment by moment.
